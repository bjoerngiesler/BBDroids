Model and assumptions

Pitch and roll plane are separate planar models with three degrees of freedom each: sphere position (rolling without slip), pendulum as a point mass below the sphere center, head as a point mass above the sphere center. The sphere is a thin shell.

Pitch plane: the drive motor acts between sphere and frame. Pendulum, IMU and head mast sit on the frame. The encoder measures the rotation of the sphere relative to the pendulum.

Roll plane: the axle tilts with the sphere (IMU roll = axle lean). The roll servo swings the pendulum relative to the axle, the dome roll servo the head relative to the axle.

Firmware 1:1: PID with measured dt, D filter 5 Hz, control term scaled by the input's controlGain (1/mmPerTick for the encoder), LibBB low-pass filters (bilinear without prewarping), encoder with 4776 ticks/rev, I share bounded in PWM. Madgwick is assumed ideal. The Dynamixel servos are modelled as a profile generator (v/a limits from the config, 0 = unlimited) with PD position control, limited by the servo's torque-speed curve. Cornering: with the axle leaning, the frame yaws at ψ̇ = (v/R)·sin ρ (sphere rolls without slip and without spin at the contact point), curve radius R/sin ρ. The centrifugal force acts on sphere, pendulum and head in the roll plane.
