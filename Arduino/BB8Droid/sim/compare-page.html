Both droids get the same stick values; each firmware maps them its own way (BBDroids: stick × drive_speed_max and × roll_angle_max; Cary: dead zone 7500, full scale at 67 % deflection, × DRIVESPEEDx and × BODY_TILT_MAX). Model

Both droids run on the same planar physics core (pitch and roll planes, shell + pendulum + head, cornering with ψ̇ = (v/R)·sin ρ, 1 kHz). Drive motor, rolling resistance and shell values are equal by default, so differences come from masses and control.

BBDroids: the sim from Arduino/BB8Droid/sim (built from the current sim.js), firmware replica at 100 Hz with balance → speed → PWM cascade, roll PID with gyro damping, I bleed and head feedforward. Cary: BB8_Drive_System V1.4.9 at 50 Hz, open-loop Sabertooth voltage with a P term on pitch and VarSpeedServo ramp, P-only body tilt on a Maestro hobby servo. Cary assumptions: 90° servo travel and 3:1 gearing for tilt and head, kinematic spin on spot.
